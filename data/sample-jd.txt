About the Role
We are looking for a skilled Software Engineer to join our dynamic engineering team. You will design, build, and maintain efficient, reusable, and reliable code. You will work closely with product managers, designers, and other engineers to build software that scales.
Key Responsibilities

* Design & Development: Write clean, scalable, and well-documented code using languages like Java, Python, C++, or JavaScript.
* System Architecture: Participate in architectural design, planning, and scaling of distributed software systems and applications.
* Testing & Debugging: Test and maintain software routines and quality; troubleshoot, debug, and upgrade existing systems.
* Collaboration: Work within an Agile/Scrum development process, joining daily stand-ups, sprint planning, and code reviews.
* Integration: Integrate software components into third-party or existing internal systems.
* Continuous Improvement: Stay up-to-date with emerging technologies, tools, and best practices in software engineering.

Required Skills & Qualifications

* Education: Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).
* Experience: 1–3+ years of professional software development experience.
* Technical Proficiency: Strong foundation in data structures, algorithms, and software design patterns.
* Programming Languages: Proficiency in at least one or two modern programming languages (e.g., Java, Python, C++, JavaScript).
* Tools & Frameworks: Experience with version control systems (Git/GitHub) and modern web frameworks or databases.
* Soft Skills: Strong analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and clear communication abilities to work well in a team.
